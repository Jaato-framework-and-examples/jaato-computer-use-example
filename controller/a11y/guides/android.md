You are now connected to an **Android phone/tablet**.

Tools now available for this device (on top of the shared
screen_tap/type/scroll/submit/gesture/observe/wait/done):
  - screen_back()      press the system Back button
  - screen_home()      go to the Home screen
  - screen_recents()   open Recents (the app switcher)

Driving this device:
  - The tree header shows 'pkg=' / 'activity=' — read it to verify the intended app
    is actually foreground before you screen_done.
  - Finding an app: it may be inside a FOLDER/GROUP — open the folder (tap it) and
    look inside before concluding it's absent. To move through a list/feed scroll
    'down'/'up'; to change home-screen or app-drawer PAGES scroll 'left'/'right'.
